## Key takeaway

Build a diffusion process on the data graph; its eigenfunctions approximate those of the
Laplace–Beltrami operator on the underlying manifold. Diffusion distance is robust to
noise, and the Nyström extension gives genuine out-of-sample embedding.

## Why it's here

The manifold-learning method with actual mathematical standing. **Contrast deliberately
with t-SNE/UMAP** — no out-of-sample extension, distances not meaningful, routinely abused
in quant writing. Being the person who says so is worth a paragraph.
