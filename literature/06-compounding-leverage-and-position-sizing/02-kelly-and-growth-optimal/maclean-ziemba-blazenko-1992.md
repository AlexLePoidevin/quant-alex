## Key takeaway

Formalises the trade-off that fractional Kelly is an informal answer to. Treats growth
rate and security (probability of reaching a goal before a drawdown level) as two objectives
and traces the efficient frontier between them, showing that fractional-Kelly strategies
sit on it. Gives the quantitative version of "half-Kelly keeps most of the growth for much
less of the pain".

## Why it's here

Turns the rule of thumb into a frontier you can pick a point on — the complement to
Merton's utility-based answer. Between them, `c = 0.5` stops being arbitrary.
