## Key takeaway

The practitioner's treatment, by someone who ran the strategy with real money in three
different games. Derives `f* = mu/sigma^2` in the continuous-time limit, works through the
discrete betting cases, and is candid about the properties that make full Kelly
unpleasant to live through — the drawdown distribution in particular.

## Why it's here

**Where fractional Kelly gets its practical defence**, and a useful corrective to the
theory's own promotional tone, from the person with the strongest claim to the theory.
