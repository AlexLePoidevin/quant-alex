## Key takeaway

The field in one binding: Kelly, Breiman, Thorp, Samuelson's attacks and the replies,
the good properties and the bad ones, plus applied chapters. Includes the standard list of
Kelly's unattractive features — the drawdowns, the sensitivity to estimated `mu`, the
long time to asymptopia.

## Why it's here

**The efficient way to survey the debate rather than only its winners.** Its structure —
good properties *and* bad properties as separate chapters — is the model for how this
article should treat the criterion.
