## Key takeaway

Firm-level shocks are supposed to average away in a large economy. They don't, when the
input–output network is **asymmetric**: a shock to a heavily-used supplier propagates
downstream and shows up as an aggregate fluctuation.

## Why it's here

The causal mechanism behind the market mode. Article 1 shows the factor exists; this
explains where it comes from, without invoking investor behaviour at all.
