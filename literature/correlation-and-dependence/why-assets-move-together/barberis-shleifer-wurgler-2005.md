## Key takeaway

Index inclusion raises a stock's comovement with the index **without changing its cash
flows**. A near-clean natural experiment: the fundamentals are held fixed and the
correlation structure moves anyway.

## Why it's here

The single most persuasive piece of evidence that part of market structure is *socially
constructed* rather than economic. Also the empirical bridge to the passive-investing
literature already in the other topic.
