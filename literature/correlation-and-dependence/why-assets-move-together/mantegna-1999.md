## Key takeaway

Turn the correlation matrix into a distance metric, build the **minimum spanning tree**,
and the industry taxonomy falls out — sectors cluster, with no metadata supplied. Market
structure is hierarchical, not flat.

## Why it's here

The best *picture* available for this series: one figure in which sector structure emerges
from prices alone. Deploy it as illustration — it is a rendering of the correlation matrix,
not an independent argument.
