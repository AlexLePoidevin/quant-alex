## Key takeaway

Applies Marchenko–Pastur to real equity correlation matrices and finds that **the large
majority of eigenvalues are indistinguishable from noise**. Only a handful escape the
band — the market mode plus a small number of sector-like modes.

## Why it's here

The canonical empirical statement of the series' central fact, and the direct template for
the figure in the first post.
