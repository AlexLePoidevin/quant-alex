## Key takeaway

Survey of RMT in finance: noise dressing, cleaning schemes, and the consequences for
portfolio optimisation — in particular that naive Markowitz on a noisy sample covariance
matrix optimises largely into estimation error.

## Why it's here

The single best entry point if you want one reading rather than four. Also supplies the
bridge to the ENB post: cleaning the matrix changes how many bets you think you have.
