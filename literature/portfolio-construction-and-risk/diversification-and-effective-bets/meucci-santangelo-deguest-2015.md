## Key takeaway

ENB depends on which uncorrelated basis you pick, and PCA is an arbitrary choice whose
later eigenvectors are unstable. The **minimum-torsion** basis instead finds the
uncorrelated set closest to the original assets, giving a more stable and interpretable
decomposition.

## Why it's here

The strongest technical critique of naive ENB, and one that rarely gets written up. If
your diversification number changes when you rotate the basis, say so.
