## Key takeaway

PCA produces *uncorrelated* components; ICA produces *statistically independent* ones by
maximising non-Gaussianity. For non-Gaussian data — which returns are — independence is
strictly stronger than decorrelation.

## Why it's here

The cleanest first step past linear-Gaussian thinking, and the one you have first-hand
experience with from the FX regime work. Decorrelation ≠ independence is exactly the
assumption the Fundamental Law's breadth term needs.
