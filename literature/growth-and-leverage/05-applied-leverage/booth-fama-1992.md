## Key takeaway

A rebalanced portfolio's **compound** return exceeds the weighted average of its assets'
compound returns, even though its arithmetic return is exactly the weighted average. The
excess — the diversification return — is the reduction in portfolio variance times a half.
It is variance drag read backwards: you cannot add to the arithmetic mean by diversifying,
but you can stop losing as much of it to `sigma^2/2`.
