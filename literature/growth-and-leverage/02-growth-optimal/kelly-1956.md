## Key takeaway

A gambler receiving noisy private signals over a communication channel should stake the
fraction of capital that maximises expected log wealth. The resulting exponential growth
rate turns out to equal the **information rate of the channel** — the criterion is derived
from information theory, with no appeal to utility at all.

## Why it's here

**Short, and the information-theoretic framing rhymes directly with `IC`.** Kelly's
result says the growth you can extract is set by how much information your signal carries
about the outcome — which is the same sentence the Fundamental Law makes, in different
notation. Read it next to `../../passive-vs-active-management/01-foundations/grinold-kahn-1999`.
