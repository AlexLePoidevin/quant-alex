## Key takeaway

Practitioners project terminal wealth by compounding the **arithmetic** mean return,
which systematically overstates what a typical investor will actually experience — the
median terminal wealth compounds at the geometric rate, and the wedge is `σ²/2` per period.
The paper documents how large the error becomes over long horizons and how routinely it
appears in professional projections.

## Why it's here

**The applied statement of the mean-median confusion, in the audience's own language.**
