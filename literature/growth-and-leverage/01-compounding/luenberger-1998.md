## Key takeaway

The bridge from the SDE to portfolio language. Luenberger sets up the log-optimal /
growth-optimal portfolio directly in investment terms — expected log return as the
objective, the resulting growth rate, and why maximising the geometric mean is not the same
as maximising the arithmetic one. Less measure-theoretic than Shreve and correspondingly
easier to lift examples from.

## Why it's here

The register this article needs: rigorous enough to be correct, written for someone
managing money rather than proving theorems. Good source of worked numbers.
