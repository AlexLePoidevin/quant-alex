## Key takeaway

The accessible overview: economics inherited the ensemble average from statistical
mechanics at a point when the two averages were not carefully distinguished, and
multiplicative wealth dynamics are exactly the case where they diverge. Argues a range of
puzzles — risk aversion, insurance, inequality dynamics — dissolve when the time average is
used instead.
