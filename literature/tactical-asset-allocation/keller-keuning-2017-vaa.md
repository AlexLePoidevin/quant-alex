## What it is

The successor to [[keller-keuning-2016-paa]]. Same breadth-momentum idea — the defensive
allocation is driven by how many assets carry positive momentum — but with a far more
aggressive response: a single asset turning negative can move a large share of the book to
cash, where PAA moves it in fractions.

The title states the thesis: the return comes from avoiding losses rather than from
capturing more upside.
