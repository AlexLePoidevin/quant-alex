## Key takeaway

Instrumented PCA. Latent factors with loadings that are functions of observable
characteristics — dissolving the distinction between "characteristic" and "factor
exposure."

## Why it's here

The linear-but-conditional midpoint between static PCA and the autoencoder. Useful for
making the point that "nonlinear" is a spectrum, not a switch.
