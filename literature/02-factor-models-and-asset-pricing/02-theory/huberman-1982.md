## Known for

A cleaner and more rigorous derivation of APT than Ross's original, with an explicit
definition of arbitrage in a sequence of economies and a tighter statement of the pricing
bound.

## Why it's here

If the article states APT formally, this is the cleanest version to state. Ross for the
idea and the citation; Huberman for the derivation.
