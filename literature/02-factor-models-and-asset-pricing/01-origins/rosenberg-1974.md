# Extra-Market Components of Covariance in Security Returns

| | |
|---|---|
| **Authors** | Barr Rosenberg |
| **Year** | 1974 |
| **Type** | Paper — *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis* 9(2), 263–274 |
| **BibTeX** | `rosenberg1974extramarket` |
| **Status** | To read |

## Known for

That securities covary for reasons **beyond** their shared market exposure, and that those
extra-market components are predictable from observable **firm characteristics** — size,
leverage, industry membership, accounting variables.

## Why it's here

The founding paper of the commercial risk-model industry (BARRA, and everything descended
from it). Important for the article because it establishes the *practitioner* answer to
"what are the factors": not statistical modes, and not macro variables, but **observable
company attributes**. A third tradition alongside Chen–Roll–Ross and PCA.

See [[connor-1995]], which compares all three head to head.
