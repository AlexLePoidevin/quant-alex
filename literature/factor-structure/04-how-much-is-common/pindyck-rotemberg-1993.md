## Known for

**Excess comovement.** Stocks in unrelated industries move together more than shared
macroeconomic fundamentals can justify, even after controlling for current and forecast
macro variables.

## Why it's here

The counterweight to a purely economic account of structure. If comovement exceeds what
shared fundamentals explain, then part of the entanglement is behavioural or
flow-driven rather than causal — which is what [[barberis-shleifer-2003]] and
[[barberis-shleifer-wurgler-2005]] later argue directly.

Useful for honesty: the pipes story explains a lot, and not everything.
