# A Variance Decomposition for Stock Returns

## Known for

Decomposes unexpected returns into **cash-flow news** and **discount-rate news** using the
Campbell–Shiller log-linear present-value identity. Finds discount-rate news dominates
variation in aggregate returns.

## Why it's here

The *economic* decomposition, orthogonal to the statistical one. A stock moves either
because expected cash flows changed or because the rate at which they are discounted
changed — and the second is largely **not about the company at all.**

This is the mechanism underneath the rates pipe, and the cleanest way to explain why a
central bank surprise reprices five hundred securities at once.
