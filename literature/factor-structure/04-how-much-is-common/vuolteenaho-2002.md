## Known for

Applies the cash-flow / discount-rate decomposition at the **firm** level rather than the
aggregate, and finds the opposite balance: firm-level returns are driven mainly by
**cash-flow news**, and firm-level cash-flow news is largely **idiosyncratic** — it
diversifies away in aggregate, which is why discount-rate news dominates at the index level.

## Why it's here

⭐ Supplies the economic mechanism for the inversion the article is built on. *Why* does the
company's own story cancel while the shared part survives? Because the company-specific part
is cash-flow news, which is idiosyncratic, while the discount rate is common to everyone.

Read directly after [[campbell-1991]] — together they explain the single-stock / portfolio
asymmetry in economic rather than statistical terms.
