## Known for

A test for the number of factors under **approximate** factor structure: if K factors
suffice, then idiosyncratic volatility measured relative to K+1 factors should be
statistically indistinguishable from that measured relative to K. Robust to the mild
residual correlation that Chamberlain–Rothschild allows.

## Why it's here

The test used by [[merville-hayes-yelken-xu-2001]] to conclude three factors — mispricing
falls from 7.66 to 0.87 and stops falling. Belongs to post 3, listed here because it is the
companion to the theory.
