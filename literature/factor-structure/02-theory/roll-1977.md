## Known for

**Roll's critique.** The CAPM is not testable, because the true market portfolio — all
wealth, including human capital, private business, real estate — is unobservable. Any test
is a joint test of the model and of whichever proxy was used.

## Why it's here

Bears directly on the question *"is PC1 the market?"* If the market portfolio is
unobservable in principle, then "the market" as used in practice is always a proxy — which
makes a statistically-extracted first component a *competing* proxy rather than an
approximation to a known thing.

Sharpens the tension between [[molero-gonzalez-et-al-2025]] (β₁ = 1 against an index proxy)
and [[merville-hayes-yelken-xu-2001]] (factor 1 loads 0.86 on an idiosyncratic-risk proxy,
so it "is not just representing the market factor").
