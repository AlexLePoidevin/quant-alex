# A Five-Factor Asset Pricing Model

| **Status** | To read |

## Known for

Adds **profitability** (RMW) and **investment** (CMA) to the three-factor model, motivated
by the dividend-discount identity rather than by data mining. Notably reports that HML
becomes redundant once the two new factors are present.

## Why it's here

Shows that even the canonical model is unsettled, and that a factor can be **absorbed** by
others — which is the clearest demonstration that factors are not fixed natural objects but
a chosen basis. Compare [[hou-xue-zhang-2015]], the competing four-factor answer.
