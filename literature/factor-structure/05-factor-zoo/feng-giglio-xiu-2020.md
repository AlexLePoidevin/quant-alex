## Known for

A model-selection procedure (double-selection LASSO) for asking whether a **new** factor
adds anything given the factors already published — rather than testing it in isolation.

## Why it's here

The methodological answer to the zoo. Relevant to the article's claim that the number of
genuinely distinct forces is small: most candidates are re-descriptions of the ones already
found.
