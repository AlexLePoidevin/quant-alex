## Known for

Coins the **"factor zoo"** and reframes the field: the central question is no longer whether
expected returns vary, but *which* of the many proposed characteristics carry independent
information once the others are controlled for. Also argues that most return variation comes
from **discount-rate** variation rather than cash-flow variation.

## Why it's here

The framing essay for the whole taxonomy problem, and it connects to [[campbell-1991]]: if
discount rates drive returns, then the dominant forces acting on a stock are by construction
not about that stock.
