## Known for

Clusters stocks by the covariance of their **market-adjusted** returns and recovers
coherent groups — commonly described as growth, cyclical, stable, and energy. The groupings
emerge from return data rather than being imposed by an industry classification.

## Why it's here

The early demonstration that **the clusters found in returns need not match the official
sector taxonomy**. "Growth" and "stable" are styles, not industries. Same phenomenon later
seen in eigenvector loadings — [[patel-2026-sp500-eigenpairs]]'s eigenvalue 7 mixes
utilities with consumer staples, which is this result in modern dress.
