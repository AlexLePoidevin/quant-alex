## Known for

Mean–variance portfolio selection. Risk is a property of the **portfolio**, not of
individual securities, and what matters is covariance rather than variance alone.

## Why it's here

Context only: the problem Sharpe's index model was invented to make tractable. Also the
origin of the idea that diversification is about **covariance structure**, which is the
whole subject.
