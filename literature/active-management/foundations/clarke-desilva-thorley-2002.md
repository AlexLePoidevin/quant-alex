## Key takeaway

Extends the Fundamental Law to **IR = IC · √BR · TC**. The transfer coefficient is the
correlation between the unconstrained "paper" portfolio implied by your forecasts and the
portfolio you can actually hold. Constraints — long-only, sector-neutrality, tracking-error
budgets, position limits — scale your information ratio down by TC.

## Why it's here

The destination of the series. The geometric reading is the payoff: **TC = cos θ**, and
every constraint is a projection onto the market's latent structure. Long-only forces
exposure to the market mode; sector-neutrality deletes the sector modes along with whatever
alpha of yours was a sector tilt.

Closes the loop opened in Part 1: latent structure ate your breadth, and the constraints
you use to manage that structure eat what's left.
