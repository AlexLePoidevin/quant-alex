## What it is

Refines [[keller-keuning-2017-vaa]] by **separating the universe that generates the crash
signal from the universe that is traded**. A small "canary" set — assets chosen for being
early indicators rather than for being held — supplies the breadth count, while the
allocation is made over a different set.

The separation is the contribution. In PAA and VAA the same assets do both jobs, which
couples the sensitivity of the crash signal to the composition of the portfolio; changing
what you want to hold changes when you de-risk.
