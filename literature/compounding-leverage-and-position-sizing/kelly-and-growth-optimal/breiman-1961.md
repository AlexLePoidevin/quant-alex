## Key takeaway

The rigour behind Kelly's claim. Breiman proves two asymptotic properties of the
log-optimal strategy: it maximises the asymptotic rate of capital growth, and it minimises
the expected time to reach any fixed large wealth target. It also asymptotically dominates
any essentially different strategy — the ratio of terminal wealths diverges.

## Why it's here

**The theorems that make "optimal" a claim rather than a slogan.** Equally important is
noticing what the theorems are *about*: they are asymptotic. Nothing here says Kelly is
good over the horizons anyone actually invests across, which is precisely the opening
Samuelson attacks.
