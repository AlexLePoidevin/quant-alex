## Key takeaway

Human judgement systematically violates classical probability — order effects, the
conjunction fallacy, interference between incompatible questions. Hilbert-space
representations with non-commuting observables model these violations natively, where
Kolmogorov probability cannot.

## Why it's here

**This is what justifies the quantum section as more than fashion.** If market structure is
partly created by how humans categorise (Barberis & Shleifer), then a formalism built to
describe human categorisation has a principled claim on this problem — not a buzzword one.

Be explicit in the writing that these are quantum-*inspired classical* methods:
Hilbert-space embeddings, density matrices, POVMs on ordinary hardware. Not quantum
computing. Readers who know will respect the distinction and distrust its absence.
