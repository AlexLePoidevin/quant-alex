## Key takeaway

Firm sizes are fat-tailed, so the largest firms are large enough that their *idiosyncratic*
shocks are macroeconomically significant. Diversification across firms fails to average
them out — the economy is granular, not continuum.

## Why it's here

The companion mechanism to the network story, and the most topical one: when a handful of
mega-caps dominate the index, their firm-specific news **is** the market factor.
