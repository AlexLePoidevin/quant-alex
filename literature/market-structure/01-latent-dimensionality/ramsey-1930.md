## Key takeaway

The origin of Ramsey theory. The pop version is the party problem: **R(3,3) = 6** — in any
group of six people there must be three mutual acquaintances or three mutual strangers.
Erdős's circle summarised the field as **"complete disorder is impossible."**

## Why it's here

Epigraph material only. Ramsey guarantees *combinatorial* structure — a monochromatic
clique in a two-coloured graph — not low-dimensional factor structure in a covariance
matrix. Different mathematical objects, and the guaranteed structure is local and weak.
**Use as analogy, label it as analogy, and let APT (Ross 1976) carry the actual argument.**
