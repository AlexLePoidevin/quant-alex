## Key takeaway

The fuller treatment: which eigenvalues deviate from RMT predictions, and what their
**eigenvectors** look like. The largest mode is delocalised — nearly every stock loads on
it, i.e. "the market." The next few are localised on identifiable industry groups.

## Why it's here

Eigenvector localisation is what turns an abstract spectrum into an interpretable story:
mode 1 is the market, modes 2–k are sectors. Pairs with Mantegna's MST.
