# Reflexivity and market history

Sources about markets as systems of people rather than as statistical objects. The
argument is that the parameters every other section estimates are partly made by the
people estimating them.

- [[soros-2013]]: fallibility and reflexivity. Market views change the fundamentals they
  are views of, so a covariance matrix is not a fixed parameter. This is a methodological
  essay, not an empirical result. The recorded lectures and interviews are listed in
  `../podcasts-and-videos/`.

A historical case of the same thing is [[mackenzie-spears-2014-formula-that-killed-wall-street]].
A correlation model became the market's price for correlation, and then failed in the
crash. It is filed with the copula material in
`../correlation-and-dependence/copulas-and-tail-dependence/`.
