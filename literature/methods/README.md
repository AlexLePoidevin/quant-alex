# Methods

Technique reading — estimation, dimensionality reduction, validation — **not tied to a
particular article.** The rest of `literature/` is organised by writing topic, which is
fragile: planned posts get cut or merged, and the reading outlives the plan. Anything here
is a tool that may serve several pieces or none.

Explainers and tutorials live here alongside primary sources. Where a primary paper is the
real citation, the note says so and links to it.
