## Key takeaway

Expected returns are linear in a small number of common factors. The argument is
**no-arbitrage**: if a large universe had as many independent risk sources as it has
assets, you could diversify into near-riskless portfolios with non-zero expected excess
return. Absence of arbitrage forces a low-dimensional factor structure.

## Why it's here

**This is the theorem the series actually rests on.** Ramsey is the epigraph; APT is the
reason structure is not optional. It says the market *must* have a shape, before any data.
