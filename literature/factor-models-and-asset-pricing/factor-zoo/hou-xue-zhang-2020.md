## Known for

Attempts to replicate several hundred published anomalies with consistent methodology
(notably value-weighting and excluding microcaps). A large majority fail to clear
significance — the widely-quoted figure is around two-thirds.

## Why it's here

The empirical counterpart to [[harvey-liu-zhu-2016]]. Together they justify the article's
posture: **a few large modes are robust; the long tail is mostly noise and construction
choices.**
