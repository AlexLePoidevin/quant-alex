## Known for

Catalogues **hundreds** of published factors and argues that, given the volume of testing,
the conventional t > 2 hurdle is far too lenient. Proposes a multiple-testing adjusted
threshold nearer **t > 3**, under which most published factors do not survive.

## Why it's here

⭐ The honest counterweight, and the same epistemics as the rest of the project: *ask what
the search process would have produced by chance before believing the result.* This is
Deflated Sharpe applied to the factor literature, and it belongs in the article as the
reason to trust a small number of large modes over a long list of small ones.
