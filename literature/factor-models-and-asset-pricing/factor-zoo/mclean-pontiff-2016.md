## Known for

Tracks published anomalies **after** publication. Returns decay by roughly a third
out-of-sample and by around a half post-publication, with the extra decay attributed to
arbitrage capital arriving once the result is public.

## Why it's here

Factors are not fixed features of nature — they **erode when observed**. That is the
reflexivity argument with a measurement attached, and it is the reason the census of modes
should be expected to change over time rather than converge to a constant.
