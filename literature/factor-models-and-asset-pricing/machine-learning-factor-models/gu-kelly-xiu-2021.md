## Key takeaway

A conditional autoencoder as a nonlinear latent-factor model: factor loadings are allowed
to depend on firm characteristics, and the whole thing is a nonlinear generalisation of
PCA fitted under a no-arbitrage restriction.

## Why it's here

The finance-legitimate nonlinear dimensionality reduction — the one that isn't just a
visualisation trick. This is what "beyond the covariance matrix" should mean.
