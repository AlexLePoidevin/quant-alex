## Known for

The **q-factor model**: market, size, investment, and profitability, motivated by q-theory
of investment. Explains a large fraction of documented anomalies and competes directly with
Fama–French five-factor.

## Why it's here

Two well-argued models, different factors, similar explanatory power, published the same
year. Good evidence that the *number* of factors is better identified than their *identity*
— a useful honest note when naming the modes.
