## Known for

Adds **momentum** to the Fama–French three factors, and shows that once you control for the
resulting four factors, most apparent mutual-fund performance persistence disappears.

## Why it's here

The cleanest demonstration of the series' closing idea: **adding a factor converts somebody's
alpha into somebody's beta.** Fund managers who looked skilled were holding a momentum
exposure nobody had named yet. Already cited in Part 1.
