## Known for

The **three-factor model**: market, size (SMB), and value (HML), constructed as returns on
mimicking portfolios. Together they explain the bulk of cross-sectional variation in
diversified portfolio returns.

## Why it's here

The empirical workhorse, and the reference point every later model is measured against.
Relevant to the article because SMB and HML are **characteristic-based** — they are neither
macroeconomic forces nor purely statistical modes, which is precisely the middle category in
[[connor-1995]]'s taxonomy.

[[merville-hayes-yelken-xu-2001]] finds its factors 2 and 3 identify with SMB and HML
respectively, so the statistical and characteristic traditions converge on the same objects.
