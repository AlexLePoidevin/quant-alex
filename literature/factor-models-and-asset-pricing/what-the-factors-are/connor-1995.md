## Known for

Sets the three traditions side by side on the same data and asks which explains most return
variation:

1. **Macroeconomic** factor models — observable economic series (Chen–Roll–Ross style)
2. **Fundamental / characteristic** models — observable firm attributes (Rosenberg / BARRA)
3. **Statistical** models — factors extracted from returns themselves (PCA)

The widely-cited result is that **fundamental models explain the most**, statistical models
are close behind, and **macroeconomic models explain the least**.

## Why it's here

⭐ **This is the direct answer to "what are the different types of factor?"** It is the
paper that organises the taxonomy rather than advocating one member of it, and it is short.

It also independently corroborates the uncomfortable finding in
[[merville-hayes-yelken-xu-2001]]: **the factors with the clearest economic meaning explain
the least variance.** Two papers, different methods, same conclusion — which makes it a
finding to build on rather than an artefact to explain away.
