## Known for

The **characteristics versus covariances** debate. Argues that expected returns track firm
*characteristics* (size, book-to-market) directly, rather than tracking **loadings** on
factors built from those characteristics — which is what a risk-based story requires.

## Why it's here

Goes to what a factor *is*. If returns follow the characteristic rather than the exposure,
then "factors" may be a convenient description of comovement rather than compensation for
bearing a shared risk. Worth knowing before asserting that the modes are economic forces.
