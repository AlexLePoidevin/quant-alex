## Key takeaway

Decompose a portfolio into uncorrelated principal directions, take each direction's share
of variance as a probability pᵢ, and define the **Effective Number of Bets** as the
exponential of the entropy of that distribution: ENB = exp(−Σ pᵢ ln pᵢ).

## Why it's here

The canonical ENB reference and the anchor for the whole post. Note it makes ENB an
*entropy*, which threads back to the eigenvalue-spectrum article.

**Keep the distinction sharp:** intrinsic dimension is a property of the *universe*; ENB
is a property of *a portfolio* relative to a chosen risk decomposition. Conflating them
turns the post to mush.
