## Key takeaway

Mean-variance optimisers are **error maximisers**. The optimiser systematically
overweights assets whose expected returns have been overestimated and whose variances and
correlations have been underestimated, because those are exactly the assets that look best
on the estimated inputs. The output looks precise and is mostly a map of the estimation
error.

## Why it's here

The general statement of which the leverage problem is a one-dimensional special case.
Useful for making the point that `f*` is not unusual in being fragile — every quantity a
covariance-based optimiser produces has the same problem, and leverage merely has the
cleanest formula to see it in.
