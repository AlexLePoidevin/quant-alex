## Known for

Correlations between stocks and the market are **higher in downturns than in upturns**, by
more than a normal-distribution model can produce. Provides a formal statistic for measuring
the asymmetry.

## Why it's here

The first crack in the linear picture, and the seed of the nonlinear posts. A single
correlation matrix assumes one number describes a relationship in all states; this shows
that is false in the direction that matters most for risk. Entanglement **tightens exactly
when diversification is needed.**
