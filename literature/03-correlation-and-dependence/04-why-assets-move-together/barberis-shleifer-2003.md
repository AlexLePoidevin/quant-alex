## Key takeaway

Investors allocate at the level of **categories** — styles, sectors, asset classes — not
individual securities. Flows therefore arrive style-wide, and securities inside a style
comove for reasons that have nothing to do with their cash flows.

## Why it's here

The behavioural half of "why the market has a shape." Limited attention forces
coarse-graining; coarse-graining creates the factors. This is the psychology vantage point,
and it is the setup for the reflexivity argument about benchmarking.
