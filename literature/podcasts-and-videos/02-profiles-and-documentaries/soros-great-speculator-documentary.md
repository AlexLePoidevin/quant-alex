## Why it's here

Biography, and only that. If it is any good it may be useful for the chronology — what he
was doing and thinking around the sterling trade, the fund's structure, how the reflexivity
framework was actually used rather than described.

It is not a source on reflexivity as theory. For that: [[soros-lectures-reflexivity]] for
his own extended statement, and [[soros-2013]] in
