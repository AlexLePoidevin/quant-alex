## Key takeaway

Title confirmed by retrieval: *"George Soros | Charlie Rose | 1994 #2."* Contents not
retrieved.

The value of the date: **1994**. Two years after the sterling trade, while he was still
running the fund actively, and fifteen years before the CEU lectures. Whatever he says about
reflexivity here he is saying as a working manager rather than as an elder statesman
defending a framework.

## Why it's here

A contemporaneous statement. The interesting question to bring to it is whether the 1994
account differs from the 2009 and 2013 ones — and if so, in which direction. A theory that
sharpens over thirty years is being refined; one that softens into generality is being
defended.
