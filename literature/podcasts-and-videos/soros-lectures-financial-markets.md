## Key takeaway

Title confirmed by retrieval: *"George Soros Lecture Series: Financial Markets."* This
resolves the video previously filed as unidentified — it is the applied lecture in the same
series whose opening instalment is [[soros-lectures-reflexivity]].

Sequence matters here: the first lecture builds the general theory, this one turns it on
markets. If reflexivity is ever going to become something testable, the argument for how it
shows up in prices is in this lecture rather than the general one.

## Why it's here

The specific claim is more useful than the general one. "Participants' views change what
they are views of" is unfalsifiable as stated; "here is what that does to a price series"
might not be. Watch this one first if the goal is to extract a measurement.
