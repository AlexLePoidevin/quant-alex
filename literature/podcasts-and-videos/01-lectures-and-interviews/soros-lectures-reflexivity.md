## Key takeaway

Title confirmed by retrieval: *"George Soros Lecture Series: General Theory of Reflexivity."*
The series is widely understood to be the five lectures delivered at Central European
University in October 2009, of which this is the first — **that attribution is from general
knowledge and was not confirmed from the page**, which returned no metadata.

The general theory, as he states it elsewhere: **fallibility** (participants' views never
correspond exactly to the situation), **reflexivity** (those views drive actions that change
the situation, so cognition and manipulation run simultaneously), and the **human
uncertainty principle** that follows — social systems have no belief-independent state, so
the regularities are not the kind physics studies.

## Why it's here

His own framing, at length and without an interviewer shaping it. [[soros-2013]] is the
compressed, refereed version; this is the extended one, and the difference in what he
emphasises when given an hour is itself informative.
